### Hi, I'm Shrishti 👋

Product manager and MBA student at MIT Sloan. I've spent my career at startups, from early stage to Series C, building products from **0 → 1** and scaling them from **1 → 100**, across **edtech, healthtech and fintech**.

I'm drawn to products people have to **trust before they'll use them**: a teacher accepting a machine's marks, a doctor or patient relying on a diagnosis, someone handing over their life savings. Getting that trust right is the whole job.

How I work: **prototype fast and let the data decide.** Faster decisions mean you fail faster and learn faster.

What keeps me in product management: at its core, it's a **creative job**. That's true at a startup or in big tech, and for a growth PM or a technical PM alike.
